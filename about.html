About

A studio built around accountability.

PRISM AI Technologies is an AI-first studio in Proddatur, Andhra Pradesh. We do consulting, engineering and infrastructure with the same team — so there is always someone who owns the outcome end to end.

Work with us What we do

Why we exist

Most businesses outside the metros get offered one of two things: an off-the-shelf product that nearly fits, or a bespoke build from a team that disappears after handover. Neither leaves you with something you can actually run.

We started PRISM to close that gap. The name is the idea — a single input refracted into a full spectrum. One business problem, examined properly, usually turns out to need strategy, software and infrastructure together. Splitting those across three vendors is how the seams appear.

So we keep them together. The person who scoped your problem is still around when it goes live, and the documentation is written so you could hand the whole thing to someone else tomorrow if you wanted to. That last part matters — a system you cannot leave is a system you will eventually resent.

How we operate

Four commitments we hold to.

These are the ones we would rather lose work over than break.

01

We will tell you not to build

The most valuable thing a consultant can say is that the project is not worth doing. It costs us the work and saves you considerably more.

02

No lock-in, ever

Source code in your repository, standard technologies, documentation written for a stranger. If you leave, everything works without us.

03

Humans review the output

AI-assisted does not mean unreviewed. An engineer checks what ships, because a system nobody verified is a liability wearing a demo's clothes.

04

Honest about limits

We say what we do not know and what we cannot promise. Rankings, accuracy figures and timelines get stated as ranges, not as certainties we cannot back.

The studio

How PRISM came together.

Origin

One problem, refracted

PRISM began from a straightforward observation: the businesses around us kept buying software that nearly fit, then paying staff to cover the gap. The name comes from what we do about it — take one input and separate it into the parts that actually need solving.

Build

Disciplines under one roof

Rather than specialising narrowly, we assembled the disciplines that a real engagement needs: consulting to decide, engineering to build, infrastructure to run it, and the design and marketing work that decides whether anyone uses it.

Practice

Engineering-led delivery

Every system is architected and reviewed by engineers. AI accelerates how we work; it does not replace the review step, and we are explicit with clients about where the line sits.

Today

Working across India

Software, cloud and marketing engagements run remotely nationwide. Interior design and on-site training centre on Andhra Pradesh, where being physically present genuinely changes the outcome.
