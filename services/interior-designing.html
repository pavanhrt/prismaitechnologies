Interior Designing

Physical space, same rigour.

Residential and commercial interiors planned with the same clarity we bring to software — space, light and material decisions made deliberately, then executed and supervised on site.

Start a project How we work

Overview

This sits beside our software work for a straightforward reason: the discipline is the same. Understand how people will actually use the space, plan for that, decide deliberately, then supervise the execution so what gets built matches what was agreed.

Most interior disappointments trace back to decisions made too late or too loosely — a socket in the wrong place, storage that ignores what is being stored, lighting chosen for a photograph rather than for reading at nine at night. Those are planning failures, not taste failures.

So we plan first. Movement through the space, what happens in each zone, where the light comes from at different hours, what gets stored where. The visual decisions come after that, and they come easier because the constraints are already clear.

The problem

What usually brings people here.

If more than one of these sounds familiar, this is the right page.

Where it hurts
  • The layout came from the builder's default plan and does not match how the space will be used.
  • Design decisions are being made one at a time on site, which is how budgets escape.
  • It is impossible to picture the finished space from a floor plan alone.
  • Contractor quotes vary widely and there is no clear specification to compare them against.
What we do about it
  • Space planning driven by how you will actually use each zone, hour by hour.
  • Decisions locked before work starts, with a written material and finish schedule.
  • 3D walkthroughs so you approve what you can see rather than what you imagine.
  • A detailed bill of quantities, so contractor quotes become comparable.

Process

How the engagement runs.

Each stage produces something you can hold, review and act on — so you always know where the work stands.

Brief and survey

How the space gets used, by whom, at what times. Plus accurate site measurements — most problems on site trace back to a dimension nobody checked.

Output: Measured drawings and written brief

Space planning

Layout options tested against movement, storage, daylight and services. We compare two or three before committing.

Output: Approved layout

3D visualisation

Walkthroughs of the agreed layout so you approve something you can see, before anything is ordered.

Output: 3D views and revisions

Material and lighting

Finishes, fixtures and a lighting plan by layer — ambient, task and accent — chosen for the hours the space is used.

Output: Material schedule and lighting plan

Execution and supervision

Bill of quantities, contractor coordination, and site supervision against the drawings, with a snag list before handover.

Output: Completed space and snag sign-off
